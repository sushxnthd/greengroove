A STUDENT PRODUCT + SYSTEMS PROJECT
GREEN GROOVE / SMART SHOPPING

A supermarket system designed around the moment a product leaves the shelf.

Green Groove combines an RFID-enabled shopper identity, smart-shelf sensing and a digital cart to rethink checkout and inventory interaction.

It began as a product-design project and developed into a broader IoT system spanning hardware concepts, interface design and retail workflow research.

Physical actions become digital state.
