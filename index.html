01The idea

A retail interaction redesigned from first principles.

The cart exists
before the counter.

Traditional checkout waits until the end of a shopping trip to identify every product again. Green Groove explores a different model: identify the shopper once, sense product movement at the shelf, and maintain the bill continuously.

The goal is not to make a faster checkout counter. It is to make the conventional counter unnecessary.

01 / IDENTITYRFID wristband

A unique shopper identity links physical shelf events to one virtual cart.

02 / SENSINGSmart shelf

Load-cell and RFID sensing detect product movement where it actually happens.

03 / CARTContinuous billing

Each detected pick updates the customer’s virtual cart instead of waiting for a final rescan.

03Architecture

Physical actions become
digital state.

A product leaving a shelf changes a measurable signal. RFID provides identity context; load cells provide physical-state context. Their combination allows the system to maintain a transaction record as shopping happens.
