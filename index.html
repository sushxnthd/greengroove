GREEN GROOVE / SMART SHOPPING

A connected retail system designed around the moment a product leaves the shelf.

Green Groove combines an RFID-enabled shopper identity, smart-shelf sensing and a digital cart to rethink checkout and inventory interaction.

This website extends the original Behance case study without introducing a second visual language: the original boards remain the primary design surface, while the surrounding web layer stays deliberately restrained.

PHASE 1DISCOVER✦
IOT IN SUPERMARKET

Understand the friction before designing the intervention.

The first phase maps the existing supermarket experience, identifies recurring operational pain points and frames the three connected product touchpoints: Smart Shelf, Groove Band and the G'G app.

01

Smart Shelf

Shelf-side sensing tracks product movement and changing inventory state.

02

Groove Band

An RFID wearable gives the shopper a persistent identity during the trip.

03

The G'G App

A lightweight interface exposes the evolving digital cart and store information.

PHASE 2IDEATE✦
PRODUCT ENGINEERING

Kano analysis before form.

Basic requirements, satisfiers and delighters were separated before visual direction was locked. The design brief then condensed the intended character into three words: modern, retroistic and minimal.

PHASE 3DESIGN✦
FROM CONCEPT TO SYSTEM

The shelf, the band and the transaction.

The designed system moves identity and sensing upstream from the checkout counter. A shopper is identified once, product movement is sensed where it occurs, and the digital cart is maintained throughout the trip.

IDENTITY SYSTEM

One visual language from product concept to final wordmark.

The identity is intentionally narrow: near-black backgrounds, white and gray typography, grayscale photography, luminous green→cyan→blue gradients, rounded geometry and neon wireframes.
