GREEN GROOVE / THE FIELD

A physical-digital retail system built around what happens before checkout.

When a product leaves a shelf, a transaction has already begun physically. Green Groove asks whether that action can become a trustworthy, reversible digital state change at the same moment.

Selected under NCERT PRAYAAS₹50,000 reported project supportIoT × retail systems

BUILT BY

Green Groove began as a student product-design project and evolved into a systems question: how do identity, sensing, association and reversibility stay consistent in the physical world?
